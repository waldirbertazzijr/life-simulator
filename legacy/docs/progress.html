Next target

Find out why the herbivores die.

Ten read-only diagnostic agents are running four lenses on the ecology (demography, energetics, behaviour, stability theory), three on the visuals, and three on engineering. No builder touches a file until they report. The leading hypothesis is that the forest is pinned flat at its carrying capacity (611 ± 21, CV 0.03) — a constant food supply rather than a prey population — which collapses the food web to plain Lotka-Volterra between herbivores and carnivores, and that always dies in a finite stochastic world.
